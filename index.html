Behind the Byline

I write real stories thanks to a background in English literature, journalism, and manual labor. I specialize in issues and topics involving hunting, fishing, and conservation, but I’ve been known to write just about anything—from squirrel taxidermy to honest gear reviews.

I’ve installed irrigation systems, dug trails as a member of Americorps, and washed more than my share of dishes. In short, I enjoy working. A little weird, I know.

I bring more than 25 years of experience to the table, having honed my craft as a newspaper reporter, freelance journalist, hunting editor, and now, as managing editor of a national magazine. As a business owner specializing in contract work, I bring that same dedication and expertise to every client’s project.
